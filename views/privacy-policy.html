Privacy Policy for Biashara Plus

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Commodity Credit Network ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Biashara Plus mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Business name
  • Phone number
  • Business location information (region, district, street)
  • Product and inventory records
  • Sales and transaction history
  • Cash flow records (income and expenses)
  • Customer names and phone numbers entered by business owners for record-keeping
  • Loan/debt tracking information entered by business owners
  • Device authentication tokens
  • Subscription payment transaction information (such as payment reference, selected subscription plan, and payment status)

2. Camera and Gallery Access

Biashara Plus requests access to your device camera and photo gallery only to allow invoice scanning, business document capture, and image selection for business-related features.

We do not use camera or gallery access for surveillance, advertising, or unrelated tracking purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate secure access
  • Manage products and inventory
  • Track sales and business reports
  • Record business cash flow
  • Help business owners manage customer debt records
  • Process subscription payments
  • Activate and manage subscription plans
  • Improve app performance and security
  • Provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure, or alteration.

Authentication tokens and sensitive account information are handled securely.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

For subscription payment processing, limited payment-related information may be shared with trusted payment service providers to securely complete transactions.

Information may also be shared when required by law or when necessary to provide and maintain our services.

6. Data Retention

We retain information as long as necessary to provide business record services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or until account deletion is requested and processed.

7. Children's Privacy

Biashara Plus is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. User Rights

Users may request account assistance, data correction, or account deletion by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
